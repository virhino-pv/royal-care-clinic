2. Laboratory Diagnostic Testing

Our diagnostic laboratory begins sample collection promptly at 7:30 AM from Monday to Saturday, and at 8:00 AM on Sundays to accommodate fasting glucose and lipid profile tests.

Fasting is mandatory for:
  • Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) — 8 to 10 hours of fasting.
  • Lipid Profile (Cholesterol, Triglycerides, HDL, LDL) — 10 to 12 hours.
  • Liver Function Test (LFT) & Master Health Checks — 10 hours.
You may drink plain water during the fasting window.

Routine tests like Complete Blood Count (CBC), Urine Routine, and Blood Sugar are ready within 2 to 4 hours. Specialized panels (Thyroid, HbA1c, Vitamin D/B12, Culture) are completed the same evening or within 24 hours. Digital copies are delivered via WhatsApp/SMS.

3. Payments & Billing

We accept all major payment modes: Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit Cards, Debit Cards, and Net Banking. GST-compliant printed and digital invoices are issued for all services.
